⭐️ Guest post from Katya Bogatyreva!

Drawing a coffee machine might be difficult, but a moka pot is simpler.

It has definitely become rooted in pop culture; when you draw a moka pot, you can almost smell the coffee.

To draw it, I visually divided the coffee maker into several parts:

1. Base, the lower part. Four quadrilaterals or curved trapezoids.

2. The arc that connects the upper and lower parts.

3. Leave space for the "spout" and draw the continuation of the four trapezoids. Add the spout and the triangular lid.

4. Handle on the side and handle on the lid.
