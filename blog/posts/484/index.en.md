---
title: ⭐ Starfish
---

For a starfish we'll need some serious preparation. If you have nothing to erase with, just do the listed steps mentally.

1. Place a dot in the center and five dots at equal distance around it. Ideally, the distances between two neighboring dots around the central one should be the same.
2. Connect the side dots to the central one.
3. Place a dot between the lines at an equal small distance from the central one.

That's it for the preparation. If you did this in your head — hopefully it worked out for you.

All that's left is to connect the far dots to the near ones with a slightly curved line, and you get a star. After that, all the auxiliary dots and lines can be erased (or forgotten).

For beauty, you can add some patterns.
