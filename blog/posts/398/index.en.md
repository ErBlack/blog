---
title: 🦠 Virus
---

Thanks to recent events, we now all know exactly what a virus looks like. Let's draw it.

We'll start with an imaginary circle. Or a circle drawn in pencil, because we'll need to erase parts later. Around this circle, let's draw several small circles and a few more inside.

The next step will be to draw legs from these circles pointing toward the center of the circle. The farther a small circle is from the center, the longer its leg should be. And each leg should be wider at the base than at the top.

Now, if your circle was drawn in pencil, erase the lines that are overlapped by the legs. If you had an imaginary circle, draw in the lines that form the circle.

Finally, add legs positioned at the back of the circle. That means drawing a few more circles with legs that connect to the main circle.

Together, these legs form a crown-like structure around the circle, which is why this virus is called a coronavirus.
