---
title: 🪁 Kite
---

Today we're drawing a kite. It's not difficult to draw, but there are some tricks.

Let's start with the frame. The frame is a cross, but since the kite is flying, its lines are slightly curved from the wind.

The skin of the kite is stretched over the cross, its edges are also slightly curved inward.

A ribbon flutters at the back as a tail. You can also add shadows and seams for beauty.
