---
title: 📎 Paperclip
---

A paper clip is, at first glance, a simple thing. A piece of wire, bent three times.

But you can't bend wire that evenly by hand. It took the Industrial Revolution and machinery to make it possible to create a paper clip. And yet its design hasn't changed since it was first made.

When drawing, the key is to turn at the right places. It took me three tries to get it.
