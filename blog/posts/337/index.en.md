title: 🛷 Sledge
---

January is coming to an end, and so are the winter themes for pictures.

It's difficult to make sleds look like sleds from above or from the side. Let's try to create an isometric projection.

First, we'll draw three small boards. If you want to add beauty, you can make them three-dimensional.

Next, from the nearest board, we'll drop down two posts to attach the first runner. We'll add the tip of the second runner, fitting it along the line of the boards.

You can also add fasteners and a small rope.
