title: Inktober
---

Apparently, a lot of people feel the need to liven things up in October. Look how many things have october in their name: [Drawtober](https://www.drawtober.com/), [Hacktoberfest](https://hacktoberfest.com/), [Orctober](https://www.instagram.com/orctober/), [Booktober](https://www.instagram.com/booktober/). And today's subject – [Inktober](https://inktober.com/). It works the same way as HowToDraw, but every day instead of every week.
