---
title: 🔫 Bullet
---

The bullet is what flies out of the pistol when fired, but we'll be drawing a cartridge. In addition to the bullet itself, the cartridge contains a case with a primer.

The primer is a small cap containing a composition that ignites upon impact. The primer ignites the powder that fills the case. The burning powder propels the bullet through the barrel, and it flies towards the target.
