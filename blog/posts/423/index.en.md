---
title: 🦋 Moth
---

A moth looks a lot like a butterfly: a bit chubbier body, slightly smaller wings, slightly duller colors, and a bit worse PR. And in Hollow Knight, the final boss is a moth too.

To draw one, start with a circle — that’s the base. From it, a triangular body goes down, a head with stylish antennas goes up, and wings stretch out to the sides.
