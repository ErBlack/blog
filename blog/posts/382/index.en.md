---
title: 📃 Visa
---

A visa is such a tricky thing, without which some humanoid cannot visit certain places.

I once had a case when I was going to a conference in Europe, and I had everything ready except for the visa. And I got my visa just one day before the conference. It was quite nerve-wracking.

A visa is a sticker on a passport page, so to draw it, we'll first draw a passport. Then we'll draw the sticker itself, add a photo, description, codes, and a stamp to it. And so that the second page of the passport isn't empty, we'll add entry and exit stamps from the country.
