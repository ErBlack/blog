---
title: 〰️ Mustache
---

Overall, you could just draw a rectangle, say it's Charlie Chaplin's mustache, and call it a day. However, the more dashing a mustache we draw, the easier it will be to tell that it's a mustache.

Start with the left side of the mustache, then add an almost identical one on the right. If you feel like it, add shading or color. And there you have a mustache.
