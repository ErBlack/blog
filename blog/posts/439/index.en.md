---
title: 🧀 Cheese
---

Today we're drawing cheese. Not every cheese has holes, but it's precisely the holes that easily transform an unknown chunk of something into a chunk of cheese, so we'll draw cheese with holes.

To start, we'll need a parallelogram (a skewed rectangle) with holes already made in it.

On top of it we'll draw a triangle where one of the sides is rounded.

All that remains is to add holes: first we'll outline those we made in the parallelogram, then add a few more in the free spaces.

We can also add shadow to one of the cheese edges, and darker shadow into the holes.
