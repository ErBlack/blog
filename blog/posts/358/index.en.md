#3

The third lecture of the "Favorite Habits" course, about how to make building habits easier.

- Habits only stick if they suit us and we understand why we need them.
- At the start, repeating the action matters more than the quality of the action itself.
- Habits joined in a chain feed each other

📖 Book recommendation on the topic: Tiny Habits by [BJ Fogg](https://www.bjfogg.com/).
