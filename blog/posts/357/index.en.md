---
title: 🔧 Wrench
---

It's better to start with the hole in the head. It's quite simple to draw: two parallel straight lines connected on one side by a curved line. The straight lines are slightly longer. The curved part is needed to fit the corner of the nut.

After we've created the hole, it's fairly easy to draw the prongs around it. From them, we continue downward to draw the handle. Make another hole in the bottom of the handle.

If desired, you can add volume.
