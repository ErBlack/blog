---
title: ⛏️ Pickaxe
---

A pickaxe is needed to extract ore in a mine or to till garden beds. On one side it has a sharp point, on the other - a wide blade. All this is attached to a handle, usually made of wood.

We'll start drawing the pickaxe with the large arc of its head. After that, we'll add symmetrical curves on the inner side, giving them volume. Then we just need to add the handle.
