---
title: 🧊 Fridge
---

A modern refrigerator in a drawing is hard to tell apart from a wardrobe, so we will be drawing a rounded retro-style refrigerator.

We will start with the door — it is a rectangle with rounded corners. Then we will add some depth: the upper part of the back wall will also be rounded, while the lower part will be straight.

All that is left is to add the handles and divide the refrigerator and freezer compartment doors.
