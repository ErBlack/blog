---
title: 🚬 Cigarette
---

Cigarettes look cool in movies. The smoke and all that. The trouble is, every year they kill far more people than wars, murders, and accidents combined. Creepy.

If there were some thing that let you blow smoke beautifully but had no other effect whatsoever, I might give smoking a try.

And drawing a cigarette is easy — a cylinder, the filter separated by an arc, and a wisp of smoke from the tip.
