---
title: 🦉 Owl
---

To draw an owl, you just need to complete two simple steps:

- Draw two ovals
- Draw the rest of the owl

If the simple method doesn't work for you - start with the eyes. Owls have large round eyes with stern eyebrows above them. There's also a beak between the eyes.

Around the face, the owl has a rather round head, below it is a fluffy body, and at the bottom is the most difficult part - feet with claws.
