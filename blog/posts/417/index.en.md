Did you also think that Ratatouille is a mouse from the cartoon? Well, no.

It's a traditional vegetable dish from Provençal cuisine made with bell peppers, eggplants, tomatoes, and zucchini.

I can't eat that today, even though I really want to.

Let's start drawing with a plate, in which we'll arrange colorful slices in a circle.
