---
title: 🕷️ Spider
---

A spider has a round cephalothorax with eyes, from which eight legs with small claws at the tips grow. At the front there are also chelicerae and pedipalps (front legs), and at the back there is an abdomen.

I start drawing with the cephalothorax, then mark the positions for the legs. Next comes the hardest part: correctly conveying the shape and length of the legs. All that remains is to draw the abdomen.
