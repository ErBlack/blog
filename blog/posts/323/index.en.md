---
title: ⛺ Tipi
---

A tipi is a type of Native American tent. It consists of straight poles arranged in a conical shape, with animal hides or canvas stretched around them. There's an opening at the top of the structure for smoke to escape, allowing for a fire to be built inside.

To draw a tipi, start with two poles crossing at the top. Then carefully add the fabric wrapped around them, making sure the smoke hole is visible. Next, draw the lower part to make it look like poles wrapped in fabric. Finally, add a rope that holds everything together at the top.
