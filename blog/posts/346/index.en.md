---
title: Два по цене одного
---

I recently decided to poke around the abandoned podcasts I'm subscribed to and found out that since October 2023 the podcast [Два по цене одного](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLVtPFfT394tEDJXWcUrOA7JTSVzJRaWKw) has come back to life and new episodes are coming out.

A long time ago, back in my life before last, I used to listen to it, and it was fun. I downloaded all the new episodes and listened to them in a couple of weeks. The podcast comes out once a week, so together with Ilya and Alexander I relived the last two years. Some events settled differently in my head, some showed a different side. It was like rereading a book.

It was also great to feel that somewhere there are people who, just like me, are trying to figure out how to approach politics, start missing food they used to be indifferent to, and every now and then get freaked out by what's going on.

My social circle is small anyway, and after moving to Belgrade it got even smaller, and here two buddies are chatting about life, and it's like I'm sitting with them. It helps.
