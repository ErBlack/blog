---
title: World Sketchnote Day
---

Every year on January 11th, the World Sketchnote Day takes place. This is because 1.11 resembles three pens.

In this case, various activities such as talks and workshops are held.

This year, Sketchnote Day is special for me. I will be giving a talk about sketches at [WSD by Sketchnote.School](https://sketchnote.school/wsd/) along with other cool sketchnoters.

The event is free, you can register via the link [https://sketchnote.school/wsd/](https://sketchnote.school/wsd/). I invite everyone to join!
