title: 🧣 Scarf
---

A scarf can be drawn simply as a ribbon with tails at the ends, but that's boring.

Let's try drawing a scarf that's wrapped around. To do that, we'll first draw a squiggle. The end of this squiggle will be the edge of the scarf.

Now let's add the second end of the ribbon to it, tucked underneath.

Finally, we'll add the tails, and we'll get a beautiful scarf.
