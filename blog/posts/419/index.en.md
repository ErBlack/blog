---
title: 🩹 Bandage
---

Drawing a bandage is very easy. It's a rectangle with rounded edges, inside which is another rectangle.

A bandage is needed to cover small wounds, and therefore it has holes so that the skin underneath can breathe.

To draw a bandage, you need to draw two parallel lines, connect them with arcs, add a rectangle inside, and draw a pattern of perforation dots on it.
