---
title: 🗑️ Recycle bin
---

The recycle bin is often used in sketches as a metaphor for something unnecessary or used up.

Drawing it is not difficult. It's just a rectangular or round bucket with papers inside.

For recognizability, you can add the recycling symbol. It consists of three arrows arranged in a Möbius strip. The symbol was invented in 1970 to denote recyclable waste. It's perfectly suited for our recycle bin.
