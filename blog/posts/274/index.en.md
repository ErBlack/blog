---
title: 🔵🦷 Bluetooth
---

Bluetooth is a wireless standard that works over short distances.

As it happens, the standard is named after King [Harald Bluetooth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harald_Bluetooth), because one of its authors was into Scandinavia and runestones at the time.

The Bluetooth sign is Harald's initials joined together (ᚼ + ᛒ). It's symmetrical. Take a vertical line and divide it into 5 equal parts, then put dots at the same distance to the left and right of the line at the ⅕ and ⅘ levels. All that's left is to connect the lines to get the Bluetooth sign.

The sign is usually drawn white on a blue background.
