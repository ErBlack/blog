⭐️ Guest post from Tatiana Morozova!

Whales are huge and powerful creatures, but at the same time, they are always kind and cheerful in children's books. Let's reflect this in different drawings.

- To depict a whale realistically, we'll draw the curves of its body in motion, add wrinkles all over the body, especially on the belly. We'll place the eyes at the base of the fin. Regardless of the whale species, we'll color it blue.
- A cartoon whale is cheerful and friendly. Its rounded body resembles a blue droplet with smooth lines, and a tall curved water spout shoots up above its head.
