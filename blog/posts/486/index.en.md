---
title: 👹 Ogre
---

Why don't we draw an ogre?

He's big, strong, and angular.

First let's draw the lip, with the fangs sticking out from under it. Below it — a heavy chin, above it — a nose, eyes under an angry brow, ears, and a bald crown.

Next — the body, arms, and legs. Here you just keep trying until it works out. The main tip — try to make them shorter, to boost the ogre's stockiness.
