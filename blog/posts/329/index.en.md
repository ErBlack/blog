---
title: 🎩 Ushanka
---

Today we'll be drawing a transformer hat. It's an ushanka. If you tie the ears on top, you can walk around looking brutally stern, like Schwarzenegger in "Red Heat," and if you lower the ears, it will be very warm.

First, we draw the front fur part of the hat. It's rectangular with rounded edges.

The next step depends on the style:

For the warm version, you need to draw the side and top of the hat and the ears hanging down with ties.

For the brutal version, draw the ears folded up and tied with a bow.
