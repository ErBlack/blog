title: 🧱 Wall
---

Today we’re drawing a wall. What makes a wall recognizable? Of course, bricks. To make the wall sturdy, the bricks are laid in a staggered pattern. We’ll do the same.

First, we’ll draw a rectangular wall. Add a concrete trim on top. Then divide the wall with horizontal lines. Split the first row into individual bricks with vertical lines. On the next row, do the same, but shift it by half a brick. This is how you need to divide the entire wall into bricks.

All that’s left is to add the shadow from the trim — and the wall is ready.
