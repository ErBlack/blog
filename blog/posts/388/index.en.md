---
title: 🪥 Toothbrush
---

A toothbrush consists of bristles and a handle. I start drawing the brush with the bristles because it's easier to maintain proportions this way.

Around the bristles is the head of the brush, from which the handle extends downward with an ergonomic curve.

The bristles are attached to the handle in tufts, which form a recognizable pattern on the cleaning surface.
