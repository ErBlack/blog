---
title: 🧂 Salt
---

Salt, like any other loose materials, looks like simple triangles in a sketch. To add volume, make the bottom line rounded.

Salt comes in different sizes: from fine to very coarse. You can convey this in a sketch too. For fine salt — straight lines, for coarse — jagged ones.

If you add a shadow, the shadow line will be just as uneven as the edges of the heap itself. And a few small crumbs around it will make it look more natural.
