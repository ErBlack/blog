#52

A wheel is a complicated thing. There's a rim, a tire, the mount, and there may also be an inner tube inside.

But at the same time it's pretty easy to draw.

Start with the rim and mark the lines of the inner and outer edge on it. Then connect them with spokes and add the mounting circles in the middle.

And draw the tire on the outside. You can add a tread pattern and lettering to the tire to make it look more interesting.
