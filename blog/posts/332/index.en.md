---
title: State of AI Interfaces
---

The first day of the workshop [Design Patterns For AI Interfaces](https://smashingconf.com/online-workshops/workshops/ai-interfaces-vitaly-friedman/) has passed. Here's what it covered:

What do people think about how AI works, and how does it differ from how it actually works?  
What control knobs does AI have, and how do they affect the generation result?  
Why doesn't AI know how much text it will write, and how does it find out the weather?
