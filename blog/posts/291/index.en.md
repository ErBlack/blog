---
title: 🍄‍🟫 Mushroom
---

The largest living creature on the planet is a mushroom weighing 600 tons. Mushrooms have saved millions of people from death. In the game "The Last of Us," mushrooms caused a zombie apocalypse. However, all this knowledge won't help us draw the most ordinary mushroom.

To draw the most ordinary mushroom, you need to start with the stem. Then cover the stem with a cap so that the top of the stem is hidden under the cap. All that's left is to draw gills on the underside of the cap and add details to taste.
