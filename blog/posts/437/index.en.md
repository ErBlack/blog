---
title: 🧺 Basket
---

I start drawing a basket with a small circle flattened at the bottom. Then I draw the handle around the top with a line, and the body of the basket at the bottom.

To make the basket look woven, you need to shade the handle with diagonal lines and the body with horizontal ones, and then insert narrow vertical segments between the horizontal lines in a checkerboard pattern so they’re arranged roughly like bricks, only narrower.
